How it works

From raw sensor data to fouling alert

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Connect onboard sensors or upload voyage logs. DP Metric accepts shaft power, RPM, speed through water, draft, trim, and position data from standard maritime data formats.

  2. 02

    Process

    Automated cleaning, outlier removal, quasi-steady-state filtering, and ERA5 hindcast weather interpolation. Draft and trim corrections applied.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    The ML fouling model and VTI calculation run on cleaned data. Change-point detection identifies the precise onset of degradation. The digital twin updates in real time across your fleet.

  4. 04

    Alert

    When fouling onset is detected, your fleet manager is alerted with estimated fuel excess, days since degradation began, and a recommended action window ahead of the next port call.
